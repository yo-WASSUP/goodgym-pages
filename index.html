隐私政策

GoodGYM 非常重视您的隐私。本隐私政策说明了我们如何处理您的信息。

数据收集

GoodGYM 不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人数据。具体来说：

  • 我们不收集您的姓名、邮箱、电话号码或任何个人身份信息
  • 我们不使用任何第三方分析或广告 SDK
  • 我们不跟踪您的使用行为
  • 我们不会向任何第三方共享任何数据

摄像头使用

GoodGYM 使用设备摄像头进行实时姿态检测，以计算您的运动次数。

  • 摄像头画面仅在设备本地实时处理，不会录制、保存或上传
  • 姿态检测完全在设备端通过本地姿态检测算法完成，无需网络连接
  • 关闭应用后，摄像头立即停止工作

面部数据

GoodGYM 不收集、存储、传输或共享任何面部数据。应用可能会根据设备端姿态检测结果在实时预览画面中显示面部遮挡模板，但该遮挡仅用于本地界面显示，不会识别身份、不会保存面部图像，也不会创建或保留任何面部数据。

本地存储

以下数据仅存储在您的设备本地：

  • 训练历史记录（每日完成的动作次数）
  • 训练目标设置
  • 语言和声音偏好设置

这些数据不会上传至任何服务器。卸载应用后，所有数据将被永久删除。

网络访问

GoodGYM 不需要网络连接即可正常使用。应用不包含任何网络请求功能。

儿童隐私

GoodGYM 不针对 13 岁以下的儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。

隐私政策更新

如果我们更新隐私政策，会在此页面发布更新内容。建议您定期查看本页面。

联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过小红书联系我们。

Privacy Policy

GoodGYM takes your privacy seriously. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

GoodGYM does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information
  • We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • We do not track your usage behavior
  • We do not share any data with any third parties

Camera Usage

GoodGYM uses the device camera for real-time pose detection to count your exercise repetitions.

  • Camera footage is processed locally on-device only and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded
  • Pose detection is performed entirely on-device using local pose detection algorithms, with no network connection required
  • The camera stops immediately when you close the app

Face Data

GoodGYM does not collect, store, transmit, or share any face data. The app may display a face mask template on the live camera preview based on on-device pose detection results, but this masking is only for local UI display. The app does not identify users, does not save face images, and does not create or retain any face data.

Local Storage

The following data is stored only on your device:

  • Workout history (daily exercise rep counts)
  • Workout goal settings
  • Language and sound preferences

This data is never uploaded to any server. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all data.

Network Access

GoodGYM does not require a network connection to function. The app contains no network request functionality.

Children's Privacy

GoodGYM is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Policy Updates

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
